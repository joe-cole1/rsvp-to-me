# Getting Started as a Host

**RSVP to Me** lets you create a beautiful event page, invite guests, collect RSVPs, and keep everyone in the loop — no spreadsheets, no group-text chaos. This guide gets you oriented; the rest of the guides cover each step in detail.

---

## Signing In

RSVP to Me is **passwordless** — you sign in with a magic link or a 6-digit code.

1. On the Sign In page, enter your email address (or phone number, if texting is enabled).
2. A one-time magic link and 6-digit code are emailed or texted to you.
3. Click the link, or enter the code on the “Check your email” or “Check your texts” screen and select **Sign in**. You can read the code on a different device.

Creating a host account sends the same link and code and lets you enter the code immediately. Use **Resend** for a new pair; registration resend does not create another account or reuse your invite code.

A few things to know:

- The link and code share a **15-minute** expiry. Using either invalidates both. Five incorrect code attempts invalidate both; use **Resend** to request a fresh pair.
- **Resend** invalidates previous credentials. On sign-in, use **Use a different email or phone** to correct your address; after registration, use **Go to sign in**.
- Administrator-created accounts receive a link-only welcome email with a **48-hour** expiry.
- Once you're in, your session lasts **7 days** before you'll need a fresh link or code.
- To sign out, click your avatar in the top navigation and choose **Sign Out**.
- To protect privacy, the confirmation looks the same whether an account exists or not. Invalid, expired, incorrect and locked-out codes show the same error. Check the identifier and request a fresh pair if you cannot sign in.

See [Your Profile & Account](faq.md) for managing your name, photo, and notification preferences.

## The Host Dashboard

After signing in, your **Dashboard** is home base. It shows:

- **Upcoming events you're hosting**
- **Past events you hosted** (for reference)
- **Events you're attending** (where you've RSVP'd Going or Maybe)
- **Pending approvals** — a heads-up when guests are waiting for you to approve their RSVP

Filter chips at the top (**Upcoming**, **Hosting**, **Open invite**, **Attended** and **All past
events**) show how many events each one holds. Chips with nothing in them are hidden, and on a
phone the row scrolls sideways.

## The Typical Workflow

Most events follow the same path:

1. **Create your event** — title, date, time, location. It goes live immediately. → [Creating Your Event](creating-an-event.md)
2. **Make it yours** — pick a theme, add a cover photo, edit details in place. → [Customizing Your Event Page](customizing-your-page.md)
3. **Set who can see it** — public, unlisted, or private. → [Visibility & Privacy](visibility-and-privacy.md)
4. **Invite your guests** — by email, text, or a shared link. → [Inviting Guests](inviting-guests.md)
5. **Manage responses** — approve guests, track headcount, read answers. → [Managing RSVPs](managing-rsvps.md)
6. **Keep everyone posted** — automatic reminders and your own update messages. → [Messaging Guests & Reminders](messaging-and-reminders.md)

Stuck on something? Start with the [Host FAQ & Troubleshooting](faq.md).

## Choosing Your Language

Use the language button (a 文A icon with the current language code, such as EN) beside your profile in the upper-right corner on any page, including sign-in. Signed-in choices are saved to your account and this browser; signed-out choices are saved only in this browser for one year. Your saved account preference wins at sign-in. An account without a saved choice adopts an explicitly selected browser language only after successful sign-in.

English is the default for missing translations. German is available as **Deutsch** and Spanish as **Español** in the language menu. Your language changes the interface and default system messages, including email and SMS, without translating event titles, descriptions, guest notes, or other content people write. Dates still use the event's timezone. Unknown email/SMS recipients receive English, while host-written messages and administrator template overrides stay as written. SMS reply commands remain `YES`, `NO`, and `MAYBE`.
